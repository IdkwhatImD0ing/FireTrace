# Code of conduct

FireTrace is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or reviews, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

- Be welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Assume good intent and explain your reasoning.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or publishing of other people's private information.
- Keep secrets out of public threads: never paste API keys, service-account credentials, or trace contents into an issue.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's report feature on the content or contact the maintainer through the channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
